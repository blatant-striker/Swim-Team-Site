WEST Mission Statement

"Coaching and guiding Children and Adults through fun, challenging, effective programs that can help individuals reach their full potential."

Learn to Swim
The WEST developmental swimming program teaches children the different strokes of swimming
Competitive Programs
The premier swim program in South Western Ontario. The WEST competitive program has produced Olympic medalists, National and World Champions
Masters Swimming
Our Masters Swimming is catered to adults of all levels looking to compete, stay in shape, or cross-train for Triathlons.
Non-competitive
Not interested in competing? Our non-competitive program allows swimmers of any level to improve their skills and confidence.
